Whether you're new to the site or a regular visitor, this page answers the most common questions about using Kamil's Unblocked — from playing games and earning XP to saving your favorites and troubleshooting access.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need an account to use the site?">
    No account is required. Everything on Kamil's Unblocked is completely free and open — just visit the site and start playing immediately. No sign-up, no login, no payment.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do games save my progress?">
    It depends on the individual game. Some games automatically save your progress to your browser's local storage, which means your data stays on your device between sessions. Others don't support saving at all. If a game doesn't seem to remember your progress, that's a limitation of the game itself rather than the site.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why isn't a game loading?">
    There are a few common reasons a game might not load:

    - **Slow connection** — The game file may still be downloading. Give it a moment and try again.
    - **Ad blocker or script blocker** — Some browser extensions can interfere with game content. Try disabling them for this site.
    - **Temporarily unavailable** — Occasionally a game file may be briefly unreachable. Try refreshing the page (F5 or Ctrl+R).
    - **Browser compatibility** — If the issue persists, try opening the game in a different browser. Chrome or Edge are recommended.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will the site work on my school's network?">
    Kamil's Unblocked is designed to work on restricted networks. That said, some games may still be filtered depending on your school's specific rules. If a particular game is blocked, try others in the library — the vast majority should be accessible.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I earn XP?">
    Every time you launch a game from the All Games page, you automatically earn XP — no extra steps needed. You start at **25 XP per game launch**, and that amount increases by **5 XP for every 10 levels** you gain. As you level up, you'll also unlock new profile avatars.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I download games?">
    Yes! On the All Games library page, each game has a **⬇** (download) button next to it. Clicking it downloads the game as a standalone HTML file you can open in any browser — no internet connection required.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did my pinned games or recently played list disappear?">
    Your pinned games and recently played history are stored in your browser's local storage. If you clear your browser data, cookies, or site storage, that information will be reset. To restore it, re-pin your favorite games from the All Games library (you can pin up to **3 games** at a time) and play a few games to rebuild your recently played history (which tracks your last **5** games).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use the site on mobile?">
    Yes, the site is mobile-responsive and will adapt to your screen size. However, some games — particularly those with keyboard controls or complex interfaces — work best on a desktop or laptop. Most casual games play fine on mobile.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Polytrack an external link?">
    No — Polytrack is hosted **directly on this site** as a local HTML file. When you click to play Polytrack, it loads from Kamil's Unblocked itself, not from any third-party server. This means it's fast, reliable, and doesn't depend on an outside service staying online.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I reset Disguise or Hide Site settings?">
    Open the All Games page and click the **⚙️ Settings** icon in the top-right area of the header. Inside the Settings panel, click **Reset to Default** to restore the original Disguise and Hide Site values. Click **Save** to apply any custom changes you've made.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
