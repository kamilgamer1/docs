If something isn't working as expected on Kamil's Unblocked, this guide covers the most common issues and how to fix them quickly. Work through the steps for whichever problem matches what you're seeing.

---

## Game Won't Load

<Steps>
  <Step title="Refresh the page">
    Press **F5** or **Ctrl+R** (Cmd+R on Mac) to reload. Sometimes a game simply needs a fresh request to start loading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Disable ad blockers or script blockers">
    Browser extensions like uBlock Origin or similar tools can block the scripts that load games. Try disabling them for this site and reload.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try a different browser">
    Chrome or Edge are recommended for the best compatibility. If you're using Firefox, Safari, or a school-managed browser, switching may resolve the issue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your internet connection">
    Make sure you have an active connection. A weak or throttled network can prevent game files from downloading correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Black Screen or Blank Game

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wait for the game to finish loading">
    Some games — particularly Unity and WebGL titles like **3Dash** — are large files that take 10–15 seconds or more to initialize. A black screen during this time is normal. Give it a moment before concluding something is wrong.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make sure JavaScript is enabled">
    All games require JavaScript to run. Check your browser settings to confirm it's enabled for this site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear your browser cache">
    A corrupted cache can cause display problems. Press **Ctrl+Shift+Delete** (Cmd+Shift+Delete on Mac), clear cached images and files, then reload the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Audio Not Working

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your device volume and tab mute setting">
    Make sure your system volume is turned up and that the browser tab isn't muted. In Chrome and Edge, you can right-click the tab and select **Unmute tab** if it's been silenced.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look for an in-game volume control">
    Many games manage their own audio independently. Look for a speaker or volume icon within the game's UI and make sure it isn't set to zero or muted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Pinned Games or Recently Played Disappeared

<Steps>
  <Step title="Understand why it happened">
    Your pinned games and recently played list are stored in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser history, cookies, or site data will wipe this information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-pin your favorites">
    Head to the **All Games** library and click the **📌** pin button next to each game you want to restore. You can pin up to **3 games** at a time. Play a few games to rebuild your recently played history, which tracks your last **5** launches.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Sidebar Not Showing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the hamburger button">
    Look for the **☰** button in the top-left corner of the page and click it. This toggles the sidebar open and closed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your saved sidebar state">
    Your browser remembers whether you last had the sidebar open or closed. If you previously closed it, it will start closed on your next visit. Click **☰** to toggle it back open.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Dark Mode Not Sticking

<Steps>
  <Step title="Re-enable dark mode">
    Click the **🌙** button on the page to switch back to dark mode. Your preference is saved to local storage, so it will persist across visits on the same browser and device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check if local storage was cleared">
    If your browser cleared local storage — due to a cache wipe, private browsing session, or browser settings — your dark mode preference will have been reset. Simply click **🌙** again to re-enable it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
