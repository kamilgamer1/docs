Kamil's Unblocked is a browser-based entertainment hub that gives you instant access to hundreds of games, movies, shows, music, and useful websites — all from a single page. Because everything runs directly in your browser, you don't need to download or install anything. The site is designed to work on school and work networks where entertainment platforms are typically blocked, so you can enjoy your free time without worrying about restrictions.

## Interface Features

The site includes a collapsible **sidebar** on the left that lets you jump between sections — Home, Games, Movies / Shows, Websites, Music, About Us, and GitHub — in one click. A **dark mode** toggle (🌙) sits in the top-right corner so you can switch to a darker color scheme whenever you prefer. Both settings are saved automatically to your browser, so the site always opens exactly the way you left it.

## XP & Leveling System

As a fun bonus, the all-games section includes an **XP and leveling system**. Every game you launch earns you experience points, which fill up a progress bar and eventually level you up. Your level and XP are tracked locally in your browser — no account needed.

<Tip>
  Bookmark Kamil's Unblocked so you can get back to it instantly. If you're on a network that blocks unfamiliar sites, use the built-in **Disguise Site** button in the games section to make the tab look like a legitimate school tool — the title and favicon both change to blend in.
</Tip>
