The XP & Levels system rewards you for playing games — every game you launch earns XP, helping you level up and unlock avatar milestones along the way. The more you play, the more your profile grows.

## How XP Works

- **Each game launch earns 25 XP** to start. That amount increases by **5 XP for every 10 levels** you gain — so at Level 10 you earn 30 XP per game, at Level 20 you earn 35 XP, and so on.
- **XP needed to level up scales gradually** using the formula `Math.floor(100 × 1.001^level)`, so early levels are quick to reach and higher levels take progressively more plays.
- **The XP bar** is displayed at the top of the All Games page, showing your current level, XP progress, and XP earned per game — all at a glance.

## Avatar Milestones

As you level up, you automatically unlock new avatar profile pictures. Your avatar updates to the latest unlocked image whenever you reach a new milestone.

| Level | Avatar Unlocked  |
|-------|------------------|
| 0     | Vintage Astro    |
| 1     | Astro            |
| 25    | North Star       |
| 100   | Bedtime Bear     |
| 250   | Star-Time Astro  |
| 500   | Starry Night     |
| 1000  | Scarlet Night    |

<Tip>
  Once you reach **Level 1100**, you unlock the avatar picker. Click your avatar profile picture at the top of the All Games page to choose any previously unlocked avatar as your display image.
</Tip>

<Info>
  Your XP total and current level are saved in your browser's local storage, so your progress persists across visits on the same device and browser. Clearing site data will reset your XP and level to zero.
</Info>
