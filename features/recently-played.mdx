The **Recently Played** section automatically tracks the last 5 games you launched and displays them at the top of the All Games page. Whether you want to continue where you left off or quickly replay a session, your recent games are always right there waiting for you.

## How It Works

Every time you click a game to play — whether from the alphabetical list, the search results, or a pinned card — it's recorded in your recently played history. You don't need to do anything manually; the tracking happens automatically in the background.

## Relaunching a Recent Game

To jump back into a game you've played before, click any card in the **Recently Played** section at the top of the All Games page. The game launches immediately, just like clicking it from the main list.

## How the List Updates

The recently played list always shows your **5 most recent** games:

- Each time you launch a game, it moves to the **front** of the list.
- If you've already played 5 different games and launch a sixth, the oldest entry drops off the end to make room.
- Playing the same game twice in a row just moves it back to the front — it won't appear twice.

<Info>
  Your recently played history is stored in your browser's local storage (`astro_recent`). It's completely private to your device and browser — nobody else can see which games you've been playing.
</Info>

<Note>
  Clearing your browser's local storage or site data will reset the recently played list. It will simply start rebuilding from the next game you launch.
</Note>
