Pinning lets you mark up to 3 games as favorites so they always appear at the top of the All Games page. Instead of scrolling through the alphabetical list every time, your pinned games are just one click away the moment you arrive.

## How to Pin a Game

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the All Games page">
    Open the All Games page from the main navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a game in the alphabetical list">
    Scroll through the lettered sections or use the search bar to locate the game you want to pin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the 📌 pin button">
    Click the 📌 button next to the game name. The button highlights to confirm the game is now pinned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See your game at the top">
    The game immediately appears as a card in the **Pinned Games** section at the top of the All Games page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Unpin a Game

You have two ways to unpin a game:

- **From the game list** — Click the 📌 button again next to the game name. The highlight clears and the game is removed from the Pinned Games section.
- **From the pinned card** — Click the **✕** button on the pinned card at the top of the page.

## Pinning Limit

You can pin a maximum of **3 games** at a time. If you try to pin a fourth game, you'll see an alert: **"You can only pin up to 3 games! Unpin one first."** Remove one of your current pins before adding a new one.

<Info>
  Pinned games are saved to your browser's local storage (`astro_pinned`). They persist across sessions as long as you're on the same device and browser — your favorites will still be there the next time you visit.
</Info>
