The **Websites** section of Kamil's Unblocked provides a hand-picked directory of useful sites you can access even on restricted school or work networks. Instead of running into blocked pages, you can use this list as a reliable jumping-off point for the content and tools you actually need. Click any button on the page to navigate directly to that site.

## Getting There

Open the **Websites** section by clicking **Websites** in the left-hand sidebar from any page on Kamil's Unblocked. The page loads a searchable list of buttons — click any entry to navigate to that site.
