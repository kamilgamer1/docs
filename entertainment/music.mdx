The **Music** section of Kamil's Unblocked lets you stream audio tracks directly in your browser without needing a music app, streaming subscription, or account of any kind. Just open the page, pick a track, and start listening — even on restricted school or work networks.

You can also use the **search bar** at the top of the Music page to quickly find a specific track by name.

## Using the Music Player

1. Navigate to the **Music** section using the sidebar.
2. Click any track button to open and play that track.
3. Use your **browser's built-in audio controls** to adjust the volume, pause, rewind, or skip through the track.

<Info>
  Tracks play directly in your browser using the built-in audio player — no external app, plugin, or download is required.
</Info>

<Note>
  Want to suggest a track for the library? Use the **Music Recommendations** form linked at the top of the Music page to submit your request.
