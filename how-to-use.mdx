This page walks you through everything you need to get started on Kamil's Unblocked — from opening the site to launching your first game or stream. The whole process takes less than a minute.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the site in your browser">
    Navigate to Kamil's Unblocked in any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari all work). No account, login, or installation is required — the site loads instantly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the sidebar to navigate">
    The sidebar on the left lists every section: **Home**, **Games**, **Movies / Shows**, **Websites**, **Music**, **About Us**, and a link to the GitHub page. Click any link to jump straight to that section. If the sidebar is hidden, click the **☰** button in the top-left corner to bring it back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse the grid or search for content">
    Each section displays content in a visual card grid. Scroll through the cards to explore what's available. On the all-games page, use the **search bar** at the top to filter games by name and find exactly what you're looking for right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click a card to launch content">
    Click any game card, movie, or content tile to start it. Featured games on the main games page play **inline** — right on the same page. The full all-games library opens each game in a **new tab**, so your library stays open in the background and you can easily switch back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sidebar Visibility

The **☰** (hamburger) button in the top-left corner shows or hides the navigation sidebar. This is useful when you want extra screen space while playing a game or watching a video. Click it again at any time to bring the sidebar back.

<Note>
  Your preferences — including dark mode and sidebar state — are saved automatically in your browser's local storage. The next time you visit, the site opens exactly as you left it, with no extra setup needed.
</Note>
