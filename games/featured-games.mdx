The main Games page is a curated grid of featured titles selected for their popularity and playability. Each card shows the game's icon and name — click any card to launch the game instantly inside your browser tab. No installs, no plugins, no logins required.

## Featured Titles

Here is what you will find on the featured games grid:

- **3Dash** — A 3D take on the classic rhythm-platformer formula, built with Unity WebGL. The game loads and runs entirely in the page, so you get smooth 3D gameplay without leaving your tab.
- **Slope** — A fast-paced, endless 3D ball-rolling game where you steer a ball down a neon slope at increasing speed. Simple controls, addictive gameplay.
- **Polytrack** — A browser-based racing and track-building game where you construct custom polygon tracks and then race them. Polytrack is hosted directly on the site for fast, reliable loading. See the [Polytrack page](/games/polytrack) for a full guide.
- **Roblox** — Browser-accessible Roblox powered by an Eaglercraft-style proxy, letting you jump into Roblox worlds without the desktop client.
- **More Games** — The featured grid also includes a card linking to the full [More Games](/games/more-games) library, which expands the selection well beyond these highlights.

<Note>
  All featured games open and play directly in your current browser tab. You do not need to download anything or allow any browser extensions.
</Note>

## Navigating Back

After launching a game, press your browser's **Back button** to return to the Games page. You can also use the **Home** link in the site's sidebar at any time to jump back to the main navigation.
