Polytrack is a browser-based racing and track-building game in which you design your own courses from polygon pieces and then race on them. Unlike most games on the site that load from an external CDN, Polytrack is hosted as a dedicated HTML file directly on Kamil's Unblocked. That means it loads quickly and reliably every time — even if the original upstream source is unavailable.

## How to Play

1. Go to the **Games** page and click the **Polytrack** card, or navigate directly to the Polytrack page from the sidebar.
2. The game loads in your browser tab within a few seconds — no download, plugin, or browser extension is required.
3. Use the **arrow keys** or **WASD** to accelerate, brake, and steer your vehicle during a race.
4. Follow the in-game prompts to enter the **track editor**, where you place and connect track pieces to build your course before racing it.

## Track Building Tips

- **Start simple.** Lay down a short loop first and test it before adding complexity. A track you can actually finish is more fun than an elaborate one you cannot complete.
- **Use curves gradually.** Abrupt direction changes send your car off the track at speed. Ease into corners by placing curved segments before and after straight sections.
- **Test early and often.** After placing every few pieces, click the play button to drive through what you have built so far. Catching a bad turn early saves a lot of rebuilding.
- **Adjust elevation carefully.** Steep hills and drops look exciting but can launch your car into the air. Smooth the gradient by inserting transition pieces between flat and angled segments.

## Why It's Hosted Locally

Many unblocked sites rely on external links that can go offline, get domain-blocked, or change their URL structure. To avoid that problem, Kamil's Unblocked hosts the Polytrack HTML file directly in the site's own file system. This means:

- The game is always reachable at the same URL, regardless of what happens to third-party hosts.
- Page load times are fast because the file is served from the same location as the rest of the site.
- You can play even on networks that block specific gaming domains, since the request goes to the same domain as the rest of Kamil's Unblocked.

<Tip>
  Press **F11** (or your browser's fullscreen shortcut) after the game loads for the best racing experience. A larger viewport makes it much easier to judge corners and plan your track layout.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Polytrack saves track data to your browser's local storage. Progress may not carry over if you clear your browser data, switch browsers, or play in a private/incognito window.
</Note>
