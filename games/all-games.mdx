The All Games library is the complete catalogue of games available on Kamil's Unblocked — more than 500 titles spanning every genre, from classic arcade games and retro console ports to modern browser hits. Games are organised alphabetically, with search, pinning, recently-played tracking, and XP rewards all built right into the page.

## Browsing Alphabetically

Every game is grouped into a lettered section — **0–9** for titles that start with a number, then **A through Z** for everything else. The narrow sidebar on the left side of the page shows every letter as a button. Click any letter to scroll the main content area directly to that section, so you can jump to any part of the alphabet without endless scrolling.

## Searching

The **search sidebar** on the right side of the page lets you filter the entire catalogue by name as you type. Enter any part of a game's title and the sidebar instantly shows matching results — up to 100 at a time. Click a result to launch that game immediately. Search is case-insensitive, so you do not need to worry about capitalisation.

## Playing a Game

- Click a **game's name button** in the alphabetical list to launch it. The game opens in a new browser tab, loaded from a single-file HTML archive so it works even if the original source is blocked.
- Click the **⬇ download button** next to any game to save the game's HTML file to your computer for offline play later.

## Pinning Favourites

Click the **📌 pin button** next to any game to add it to your **Pinned Games** section at the top of the page. You can pin up to **3 games** at a time. Pinned games persist between visits via browser local storage, so your favourites are always one click away. To remove a pin, click the **✕** button on the pinned card, or click the pin button again in the main list.

## Recently Played

The **Recently Played** section at the top of the page automatically tracks the last **5 games** you launched. Each entry shows as a clickable card so you can relaunch a game instantly without scrolling back through the alphabet. Recently played history is stored in your browser — it updates the moment you open any game.

<Info>
  Every game you launch earns you **XP** displayed in the progress bar near the top of the page. You start at **25 XP per game**, and your reward increases by 5 XP for every 10 levels you gain. XP and level data are stored in your browser — no account needed.
</Info>

<Tip>
  The search sidebar also has **Disguise Site** and **Hide Site** buttons. **Disguise Site** changes the browser tab's title and favicon to something inconspicuous (configurable in Settings ⚙️). **Hide Site** overlays the page with an innocuous-looking site in a full-screen iframe — click anywhere to dismiss it.
</Tip>
